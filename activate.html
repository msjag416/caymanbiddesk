One-time form activation

This page exists only to switch on form delivery. Fill it in, press the button, tick the "I'm not a robot" box on the page that follows, then check your inbox for a message from FormSubmit and click the link inside it.

Once that's done, delete this file from your repository. It isn't part of the website and search engines are told to ignore it.

After you click the link in FormSubmit's email, go back to the website, send a normal enquiry through the contact form, and it should arrive in your inbox.
