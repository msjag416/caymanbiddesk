Effective 18 Sep 2026
CIMA · Published 20 July 2026

Rule on Effective Compliance Programme for the Prevention and Detection of ML, TF and PF

Regulator
Cayman Islands Monetary Authority
Effective
18 September 2026
Applies to
CIMA registered and licensed financial services providers

The rule sets binding minimum standards for an AML/CFT/CPF compliance programme, including a documented governance framework, defined roles and lines of accountability, and a documented risk based approach. Requirements previously addressed through guidance become enforceable obligations.

Cayman Bid & Compliance Services commentary: the documentation and evidence burden lands in the weeks before the effective date. Requirement mapping and evidence organisation are the two items most often left incomplete.

Official source: the rule (PDF, CIMA)

Effective 18 Sep 2026
CIMA · Published 20 July 2026

Rule on Compliance with Financial Sanctions and Targeted Financial Sanctions

Regulator
Cayman Islands Monetary Authority
Effective
18 September 2026
Applies to
CIMA registered and licensed financial services providers

Published alongside the AML rule following industry consultation, this rule sets binding requirements for sanctions and targeted financial sanctions compliance, including screening obligations. Both rules take effect 60 days after publication in the Gazette.

Cayman Bid & Compliance Services commentary: sanctions screening records are frequently held across separate systems. Consolidating that evidence into a single reviewable package takes longer than firms expect.

Official source: the rule (PDF, CIMA)
